Search Tips & Guides

Learn how to effectively search our library resources and get the most out of your research.

Quick Search Tips

Improve Your Search Results

  • Use quotation marks for exact phrases: "renewable energy"
  • Use AND to combine terms: solar AND energy
  • Use OR to expand your search: university OR college
  • Use NOT to exclude terms: energy NOT nuclear
  • Use an asterisk (*) for word variations: comput* finds computer, computing, computation
  • Use parentheses to group terms: (climate OR weather) AND change
  • Check spelling if you get few or no results
  • Try synonyms if your search terms aren't working

Suggest New Resources

Is there a particular book, journal, database, etc. we don't have that would make a useful addition to the library? Do you think we need more copies of an important textbook?

The MCAST library aims to make books and other resources available to support your learning and research. If the library does not have the material you require, you can suggest that we purchase it.

Request Status Tracking

You can follow the status of your request by logging into your online My Library Account and clicking on the Purchase requests button.

Making a Suggestion

Suggestions can be made by both MCAST staff and students.

Before submitting a suggestion, please use the online library catalogue to make sure that the library does not already have the item. For guidelines on how to do the search, please see Search Tips.

How to Suggest a Purchase

1

Check the Catalogue

Search our online catalogue to ensure we don't already have the item you're requesting.

2

Choose Your Method

Use the online form for books, or email us for other types of materials.

3

Submit Your Suggestion

Fill out the appropriate form with all necessary details about the resource.

4

Track Your Request

Monitor the status of your suggestion through your My Library Account.

Suggestion Methods

For a suggestion for the purchase of:

📚 Books

You can either:

  • Use our online book suggestion form
  • Download the form from here

Note: Use a separate form for each item suggested when using the online form.

📄 Other Materials

For journals, databases, or other types of resources:

  • Send an email to llrc@mcast.edu.mt

What Happens Next?

We will consider all suggestions, bearing in mind their suitability as well as any budget constraints that may be in place. Each suggestion is evaluated based on:

  • Relevance to MCAST curriculum and research
  • Current collection gaps
  • Available budget
  • Demand and potential usage

Library Opening Hours

Find opening times and contact information for all MCAST library locations across Malta and Gozo.

Important Notice

Please note the following general conditions:

  • The Main Library in Paola operates Monday through Friday, with regular hours from 7:15 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The library offers extended hours from October to May, remaining open until 7:30 p.m.
  • During the summer break (July to mid-September) and certain holiday periods, the library's hours may be adjusted, closing at 1:30 p.m.
  • The main and offsite libraries are closed on weekends, public holidays, and during the MCAST Christmas shutdown.
  • Offsite libraries are also closed during the Summer term recesses.
  • Offsite libraries will be closed on days when the designated staff member is on leave. We recommend that visitors from outside the respective campuses call to confirm the library's availability before planning their visit.
